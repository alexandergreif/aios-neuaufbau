# Personal Weekly Review

Follow the [core rules](../../rules/core.md), [storage](../../references/storage.md) and for personal details [profile and knowledge](../../references/profile-and-knowledge.md). Use only tasks, calendar entries and, when needed, specifically selected session histories approved for this review. If a needed source is missing, ask first about how to proceed and wait for the decision before judging on a limited basis.

Discuss what was achieved, open commitments, obstacles and personal working style. Give constructive feedback on strengths and areas for improvement using concrete examples. Separate observed events from your interpretation. Missing search hits, tasks that have gone quiet or earlier plans do not substantiate completion. Name important source gaps and do not judge a person based on unsubstantiated guesses.

Suggest several possible improvements and one concrete, small weekly experiment. Choose at most one focus for the coming week together with the person. At a later review, ask about the actual effect instead of assuming success. Do not change tasks, profile or rules automatically. The personal review is separate from [weekly planning](../weekly-planning/SKILL.md) and [system retro](../system-retro/SKILL.md), runs only on invocation and does not include a routine model check.

[Synthetic test cases](references/test-cases.md) describe later behavior checks, not passed model tests.
