# Weekly Planning

Follow the [core rules](../../rules/core.md), [storage](../../references/storage.md) and for working hours and time zone [profile and knowledge](../../references/profile-and-knowledge.md). Draw only on the approved sources needed for this week. If a needed source or capability is missing, ask how to proceed and wait for the decision before creating a limited plan.

Determine confirmed priorities, your own open tasks with evidenced deadlines and the available calendar. Use working hours and time zone from the current profile, where available. Distinguish fixed appointments, absence and your own movable work blocks. Count overlaps only once and show free capacity only when the calendar is sufficiently complete. Allow for realistic buffers without a rigid utilization limit. Separate real deadlines from planning days you proposed yourself. Name scheduling conflicts, overload and missing information.

By default, deliver a weekly proposal with focus areas, next steps and suitable time slots. An existing personal weekly review can feed in on request but is not a prerequisite. "Plan my week" is not a task to change the calendar or tasks. Carry out specifically requested changes within their scope, check the affected calendar or task right before doing so and read back the saved result. Do not blindly repeat actions when the outcome is uncertain. Do not start automatic weekly planning.
