# Research

Follow the [core rules](../../rules/core.md), [storage](../../references/storage.md) and for lasting knowledge notes [profile and knowledge](../../references/profile-and-knowledge.md). Determine question, purpose, time frame and desired output format from the task. Clarify only essential ambiguities. With a clear task, start directly. If a source or access needed for the answer is missing, name the gap, ask first about how to proceed and wait for the decision before continuing on a limited basis.

Choose depth, sub-questions and source scope based on the question and a given time budget. Search iteratively: read initial sources, check decisive statements, identify gaps and contradictions and research further in a targeted way. Prefer suitable primary sources and check their date, scope and evidential strength. Actively look for counterarguments and divergent findings. Multiple copies of the same source are not independent evidence.

Link important statements to verifiable source anchors. Separate facts, interpretation and forecast. Show unresolved contradictions and uncertainty along with their practical significance. State conclusions only as strongly as the evidence supports. Do not use invented quotes or placeholder sources. The length and form of the result follow the task, without a fixed source quota or blanket plan approval. A research task sends nothing and does not turn results into lasting knowledge rules without confirmation.
